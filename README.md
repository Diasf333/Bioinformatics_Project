# Bioinformatics_Project

# Improving Surface Enhanced Raman Spectroscopy (SERS) Preprocessing and Analysis

**Bioinformatics MSc Project – University of Minho**

---

## 📖 Abstract

Antibiotic residues, particularly sulfonamides, are recognized as emerging environmental contaminants due to their widespread usage in human and veterinary medicine, and their persistence in aquatic environments. Traditional detection techniques for antibiotic residues are often limited by high costs, complex procedures, and lack of field applicability. Raman spectroscopy, particularly Surface-Enhanced Raman Spectroscopy (SERS), offers a promising alternative, providing rapid, non-destructive, and sensitive molecular analysis. This project aims to establish a robust preprocessing pipeline for Raman spectra, optimizing signal quality and enabling downstream analysis using machine learning. Ultimately, the integration of SERS and advanced computational tools facilitates the detection of antibiotic residues in water matrices and sets the stage for the development of intelligent, automated systems in environmental monitoring.
